# Research Student Guides

Short, practical guides for research students, distilled from the feedback I find myself giving most often when supervising projects in surgical data science and clinical AI. Most of it applies to any research project that reports a study. Your own programme's handbook and marking criteria always take precedence.

## How these guides were written

These guides are distilled from feedback I have given students over several years, in comments on drafts and in email. I used an AI assistant (Claude) to work through that feedback, identify the themes that came up repeatedly, draft the text, and find supporting references. I edited the drafts, checked the sources, and decided what to keep and what to cut. The judgements are mine, and I stand behind them.
